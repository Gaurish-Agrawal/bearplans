Product timeline

BearPlans Growth

A simple month-by-month record of the scheduler's growth, starting with the first public launch notes and continuing with current site updates.

Aug 2024

First public scheduler launch

BearPlans launched as a WashU schedule planner for exploring conflict-free class combinations before registration.

Sep 2024

Reddit feedback session

Public feedback from the WashU Reddit community shaped early fixes, usability changes, and feature priorities.

Nov 2024

80 users milestone

The project reached 80 users during its first registration-season run.

Feb 2025

Multiple free-time options

Schedule preferences expanded with support for multiple unavailable time windows.

May 2025

WebSTAC to Workday move

BearPlans began adapting to WashU's registration move from WebSTAC to Workday.

Jun 2025

Feature extravaganza

Filter by professor, Rate My Professors support, share-with-friends tools, and dark mode were added. Walking distance was archived to keep the scheduler focused and faster.

Aug 2025

One-year anniversary

BearPlans marked one year of helping WashU students compare possible schedules.

Jan 2026

480 users milestone

The planner reached 480 users as usage continued growing across registration cycles.

Feb 2026

Algorithm speed revamp

The schedule generation algorithm was rebuilt for faster searching, better pruning, and more responsive results.

Mar 2026

Official WashU ONE integration

BearPlans was added to WashU ONE, making it easier for students to discover from official campus tools.

Jun 2026

Workday extension and dark mode

The Chrome extension workflow was added for importing Workday course sections directly, alongside dark mode work and the updated timeline.
